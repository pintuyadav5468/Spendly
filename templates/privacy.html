Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and category breakdowns Spendly provides. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services for marketing purposes. Where third-party infrastructure (such as hosting or email delivery) is used to operate the Service, those providers only process data as needed to provide their service to us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account settings page.
